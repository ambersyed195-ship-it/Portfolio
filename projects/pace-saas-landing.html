Project Overview

Pace is a marketing landing page designed the way a real SaaS product would launch one — built to convert visitors into sign-ups. It includes an interactive product demo widget so visitors can see the product in action before committing, along with social proof and a clear feature breakdown.

Key Features

  • Customer testimonials section for social proof

  • Smooth scroll and entrance animation effects
